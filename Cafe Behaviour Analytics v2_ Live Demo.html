Everything here is simulated: people, paths and behaviours are generated with simple rules, so the numbers show how the system works, not how real customers behave. The real project works on anonymous tracks from video. It does not use faces, age estimation or biometric identity; repeat visits come only from loyalty IDs customers choose to give at the counter.
